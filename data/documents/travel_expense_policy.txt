TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Employees traveling for approved business purposes may claim eligible transportation, accommodation, and meal expenses.

Business travel must normally be approved by the employee's manager before the trip.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days after returning from a business trip.

Receipts should be retained for reimbursable expenses.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.

Employees should choose reasonable and cost-effective travel options.
