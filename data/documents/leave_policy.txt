LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave requests should normally be submitted at least 3 working days before the planned leave.

2. Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

For sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive working days, appropriate documentation may be required.

3. Parental Leave

Employees who become parents are eligible for 16 weeks of parental leave.

Parental leave may be taken continuously or divided into multiple periods subject to manager approval.

4. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave for exceptional personal circumstances.

Approval depends on business requirements and manager approval.
