EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to complete
8 working hours per day.

2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year.

Employees should submit leave requests at least 3 working
days before the planned leave whenever possible.

3. Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

For sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive working days,
employees may be required to provide appropriate documentation.

4. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with
manager approval.

Remote work must not affect scheduled meetings, collaboration,
or required availability.

5. Parental Leave

Employees who become parents are eligible for 16 weeks of
parental leave.

The leave may be taken continuously or, subject to manager
approval, divided into multiple periods.

6. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice each year.

Employees receive feedback from their manager and may also
receive peer feedback.

7. Travel Expenses

Employees traveling for approved business purposes may claim
eligible transportation, accommodation, and meal expenses.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days
after returning from the business trip.
